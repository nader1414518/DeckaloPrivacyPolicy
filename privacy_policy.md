# Privacy Policy for Deckalo

**Last updated: 8 October 2026**

Level-Up Developer ("we", "us") built the Deckalo app (the "App"), a free app with in-app subscriptions that turns a prompt into an editable PowerPoint presentation. This policy explains what information the App and our server handle, why, and what choices you have. If you use the App, you agree to the practices described here.

**The short version:** Deckalo has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or advertising ID. Your presentations stay on your device. What you type to create or change a presentation is sent to an AI service to produce the result. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Information we handle

**1. What you ask the AI to write.**
When you create a presentation or ask the AI to change a slide, the App sends the text you typed (your prompt, audience, tone, language, slide count), the names and types of any files you attach (we do not read the contents of attached files), and, for AI edits, the text of the slide or selection being changed. This is sent to our server and then to a third-party AI provider so it can write the result. Please do not include confidential or personal information in prompts that you do not want processed this way.

**2. A random device identifier.**
The App creates a random ID the first time it runs and keeps it in your device's secure storage. It is not your advertising ID and is not linked to your name or any account. Our server uses it only to count your free presentation and to apply fair-use limits. On iPhone and iPad it may remain if you reinstall the App.

**3. Subscription information.**
Purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your payment card or billing details. To confirm that a subscription is active, the App sends the signed subscription record that Apple or Google gives the App to our server, which checks it. This record identifies the product and its dates, not you.

**4. Server logs.**
Like any internet service, our server automatically records technical data such as your IP address, the time of the request and the type of request, for security and to prevent abuse. These logs do not contain the text of your prompts. They are deleted automatically after about 14 days.

**5. Pictures and videos for your slides.**
Photos and videos you pick from your library, camera or Files, and icons, are added to your slide on your device. They are not uploaded to us. If you choose **stock photos**, the words you search for (or that the AI suggests for a slide) are sent to our server, which forwards only those words to the photo service Pexels; your device then downloads the chosen photo from Pexels' servers, so Pexels can see your IP address as for any download.

**6. Your presentations.**
Presentations you create or import are stored on your device only. We do not store them on our servers. If you import a .pptx file, it is read on your device and is not uploaded. If you export or share a file, it goes only where you choose to send it.

## How we use information

- To generate and edit presentations you request.
- To give each device its one free presentation and to verify subscriptions.
- To keep the service secure and to prevent abuse and excessive use.
- To provide support if you contact us.

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

## Service providers who process information

- **Photo service (Pexels).** Provides free stock photos. It receives only the search words from our server, and your device contacts it directly to download a photo you choose. See Pexels' privacy policy.
- **AI provider (DeepSeek).** The text described in section 1 is processed by DeepSeek to produce the AI output. Our server sends it only the text, not your device ID, IP address or any other identifier. DeepSeek acts under its own privacy policy and may process data on servers outside your country, including in China.
- **Hosting.** Our server is hosted with OVHcloud in Europe (Germany).
- **Apple and Google.** They process purchases and subscriptions under their own privacy policies.

We have no other third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising or crash reporting in the App.

## Retention

- Our server keeps a small usage record per random device ID (whether the free presentation was used, and daily request counts) for as long as needed to enforce the free-presentation and fair-use limits.
- Server logs are kept for about 14 days.
- We do not keep the text of your prompts or photo searches. The AI provider may keep prompts according to its own policy.
- Presentations remain on your device until you delete them or delete the App.

## Your choices and rights

- You can delete any presentation in the App, or delete the App to remove all data stored on your device.
- Because we do not hold an account or personal profile for you, there is usually nothing further we can look up or delete. If you believe we hold information about you, or want to ask about access, correction or deletion (including under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA or similar laws), contact us and we will help as far as we are able to identify the information.
- You can cancel a subscription at any time in your Apple ID or Google Play settings.
- If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

## Cookies

The App does not use cookies.

## Security

We use commercially reasonable measures to protect information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) between the App and our server and restricted access to our server. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information to us, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.
